## Cookies

RETINA uses only cookies that are strictly necessary for the service to work and stay secure. We do not use cookies for advertising, analytics, or tracking, and we do not load third-party analytics or advertising scripts. Because every cookie we use is strictly necessary, we do not ask for consent before setting them.

### Cookies we set

| Cookie | Purpose | When it is set | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `auth_token` | Keeps you signed in to your account. It contains a signed session token that identifies your account to our servers. | Only when you sign in using a sign-in link | 7 days, or until you sign out |

We set this cookie so that only our servers can read it: browser scripts cannot access it (`HttpOnly`), it is only sent over encrypted connections (`Secure`), and it is not sent with requests from other websites (`SameSite=Lax`). If you browse the public map or data archive without signing in, we set no cookies.

### Cookies set by Cloudflare

Our sites are served through Cloudflare, which protects them from automated abuse and controls access to the administrator console. Cloudflare sets the following strictly necessary cookies:

| Cookie | Purpose | Who receives it | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `cf_clearance` | Security: helps Cloudflare tell legitimate visitors apart from automated bot traffic. | All visitors | Up to 1 year |
| `CF_AppSession` | Keeps track of a sign-in attempt to the administrator console. | Administrators only | 24 hours |
| `CF_Authorization` | Confirms that an administrator has signed in and is allowed to use the administrator console. | Administrators only | 24 hours |
| `CF_Device` | Prevents abuse of the administrator sign-in process. | Administrators only | 30 days |

Cloudflare does not use these cookies to track you across websites. For more information, see [Cloudflare's cookie policy](https://developers.cloudflare.com/fundamentals/reference/policies-compliances/cloudflare-cookies/) and [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

### Preferences stored on your device

The RETINA dashboard saves your display preferences in your browser's local storage, such as light or dark theme, map layers and map style, and whether the sidebar is collapsed. This information stays on your device, is never sent to our servers, and is only saved once you change a setting.

### Your choices

- **Sign out.** Signing out deletes the `auth_token` cookie.
- **Browser settings.** You can view, block, or delete cookies and local storage in your browser's settings. If you block cookies, you will not be able to sign in, and Cloudflare may ask you to complete a security check more often.
- **Clearing preferences.** Clearing your browser's site data for RETINA resets your display preferences to their defaults.

If we change the cookies we use, we will update this section.
